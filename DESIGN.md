Design
Problem (P4)
Adaptive tutor with automatic question generation

Design an adaptive tutor that teaches the selected topic by generating
questions based on the learner's performance, misconceptions and
previous responses. The tutor should support different question formats,
evaluate the learner's explanations, identify misconceptions, and adjust
the difficulty or type of support accordingly.
Topic (T5)
Ratio and proportion in recipes and maps

The learning activity will focus on understanding ratios and equivalent
proportions through practical examples: - Scaling ingredient quantities
in recipes. - Using map scales to calculate actual distances.
Learner Context (L5)
Learner repeating the topic after failing it once, with high anxiety
and low self-efficacy

The learner has previously struggled with the topic and may lack
confidence when answering questions. The tutor should provide a
supportive, non-judgmental learning experience, use simple explanations,
and give encouraging feedback. It should identify the learner's
misconceptions and provide step-by-step support instead of simply
marking answers wrong.
Design Constraint (C4)
10-minute sessions
  Constraint                          Requirement
  10-minute sessions                  One complete learning sequence must
fit within 10 minutes. The learner
must be able to stop and resume
from where they left off.
The application will divide learning into short activities and save the
learner's progress after each step so that the learner can continue
later without starting again.
