## **Problem Description**

Design an adaptive tutor that teaches ratio and proportion using practical examples from recipes and maps. The tutor generates questions based on the learner's performance, previous responses and misconceptions. It evaluates answers, identifies learning gaps and adapts the difficulty or provides additional explanations based on the learner's needs.

## **Learning Topic**

The application will cover:

- Understanding ratios and simplifying ratios.
- Equivalent ratios and proportions.
- Scaling ingredient quantities in recipes.
- Understanding map scales and calculating actual distances.

## **Learner Context**

The learner has previously failed to understand the topic and may feel anxious while attempting questions. The application will use simple explanations, encouraging feedback and step-by-step guidance. It will avoid negative feedback and allow learners to practise at their own pace.

## **Design Approach**

The tutor will initially assess the learner's understanding through a few basic questions. Based on the responses:

- **Correct answers:** Generate slightly harder questions.
- **Incorrect answers:** Identify the possible misconception and provide an alternative explanation.
- **Repeated difficulties:** Revisit prerequisite concepts and provide guided practice.

The tutor will support multiple question formats, including multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blanks and open-ended questions.

## **Session Structure**

The complete learning sequence will fit within 10 minutes:

1. Quick assessment.
2. Concept explanation.
3. Adaptive question practice.
4. Personalised feedback.
5. Short final assessment.

The learner's progress will be saved after each activity so they can leave and resume from the same point.
